Treadline Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026 · Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Trailside Table, LLC

The short version: Treadline turns your photo library into trip movies, and it does that on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no sale or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Your trips are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, where we cannot read them. The only data that reaches other companies' servers is (1) map coordinates used to draw routes and name places, sent without any account or identifier from us, and (2) optional crash and usage diagnostics that contain no photos, locations, place names, or dates and are never linked to your identity. Those requests travel from your device, so the receiving companies see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Treadline is an iOS app made by Trailside Table, LLC ("Treadline", "we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the limited personal data described in this policy that we actually receive. This policy explains what information the Treadline app handles, what leaves your device, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Treadline app only; it does not cover Apple's own services or the third-party services linked below, each of which has its own policy.

Questions or requests can be sent to feedback@trailsidetable.com.

2. Information we handle, and how we use it

2.1 Your photo library (processed on your device)

Treadline asks for read-only access to your photo library so it can discover trips from your photos' timestamps and location metadata, and play them back as animated map tours. All of this happens on your device:

  • What is read: photo and video timestamps, GPS metadata, favorite status, and thumbnails, in order to group photos into trips and stops.
  • What is never done: the app never modifies, adds, or deletes anything in your library. It is built with a safeguard that prevents any photo-modification code from shipping.
  • Analysis: the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to pick the best photos for a tour (sharpness, exposure, whether faces are well lit). This is quality scoring, not facial recognition: it does not identify who is in a photo, and no analysis results leave your device.

Your photos, videos, and photo metadata are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The only way imagery leaves your device is when you export a trip movie and choose to share or save it (see section 2.7).

2.2 Apple Health workout routes (processed on your device)

If you choose to import a workout, Treadline asks for read-only access to specific Apple Health data so it can draw the real trail you walked, hiked, ran, or cycled on your trip map. Specifically, the app reads:

  • Workouts: the workout type, its start and end time, and its total walking, running, or cycling distance.
  • Workout routes: the GPS route recorded for the workout.

Treadline never writes to Apple Health, and reads nothing else from it (no heart rate, steps, or other health measurements). Workout data is used solely to draw routes on your trip map. It is not used for advertising, marketing, or any form of data mining, and it is not shared with third parties. When you import a workout route, its coordinates become part of that trip's route geometry on your device. Workout routes are never copied to iCloud: when a trip syncs (section 2.4), the synced copy carries only a reference to the workout, not the route itself, and any other device of yours restores the route by reading the same workout from its own Apple Health data, with your permission on that device. Our diagnostics (section 2.6) record only that an import or restore happened; they never include workout routes, distances, times, or any other Health data.

2.3 Files you import

You can also import a GPX route file from another app or service (for example, a Garmin, Strava, or AllTrails export). The file is read on your device, its route is added to your trip as described in section 2.4, and the file is not uploaded to us.

2.4 Your trips (stored on your device and in your iCloud account)

The trips you create consist of stop locations and place names, dates, trip titles, references to the photos you chose, route geometry (including any imported GPX routes and, on the device where you imported them, workout routes), and your presentation settings (theme, music selection, and similar). This data is stored in a private database on your device.

If your device is signed in to iCloud, Treadline also stores a copy of this trip data in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so your trips stay in sync across your devices. That copy lives in your Apple account, not on any server of ours; we have no access to it and cannot read, retrieve, or delete it. Apple protects it under the iCloud Terms and Apple's privacy policy, and encrypts it in transit and at rest. Your photos and videos themselves are never copied to iCloud by Treadline; the synced data holds only references to them. Workout routes from Apple Health are likewise never copied to iCloud (section 2.2); the synced data holds only a reference to the workout. You can turn iCloud sync off for Treadline or delete the synced data at any time (see section 6).

2.5 Map coordinates sent to build routes and name places

To draw routes between your stops and give stops readable place names, the app sends coordinates derived from your photos' location metadata or from an imported route to mapping services. The app never reads your device's live location; it has no access to Location Services at all.

  • Apple Maps services (map display, directions, reverse geocoding, and place search), covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • openrouteservice (operated by HeiGIT gGmbH, Germany) and the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation), used to look up hiking-trail and ferry route geometry for the relevant legs of a trip. See the HeiGIT privacy policy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

These requests carry coordinates and nothing about you from us: no name, no account, no photo content, and no identifier that links the request to you. We do not store your coordinates on any server of ours. A request still travels from your device to those providers, so they receive your IP address, and what they do with it is governed by their policies linked above rather than by this one.

2.6 Diagnostics and usage data (optional)

So we can fix crashes and understand which features work, the app can send diagnostic and usage telemetry to Datadog, our monitoring provider. This includes crash reports, performance measurements (for example, how long a route lookup took), which screens are used, and feature-usage events (for example, "a tour was exported").

This telemetry is filtered on your device before it is sent. It never includes your photos, photo identifiers, coordinates, place names, trip titles, trip dates, or Health data. It is not linked to your identity: there are no accounts, and we never attach a name, email address, or other personal identifier. It does include a randomly generated session and app-install identifier (used only to group events from the same install), your device model and OS version, the app version, and Datadog receives your IP address with the request. Because the install identifier persists across sessions, this data is pseudonymous rather than anonymous: it is not tied to who you are, but events from a single app install can be grouped together.

Diagnostics are on by default and you can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Share usage & diagnostics. Turning them off stops collection immediately. We do not use this data for advertising, do not share it with data brokers, and do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2.7 Exported movies

When you export a trip movie, the video is rendered entirely on your device. It contains your chosen photos and clips, the animated map of your trip, and place-name titles, so treat it like any personal video. If you added a workout route from Apple Health to the trip, the map in the movie shows that route too. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination in the share sheet (Messages, AirDrop, Save to Photos, and so on); sharing the movie is your decision and your act. We never receive a copy.

2.8 Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed to do so.

3. What we don't do

  • No user accounts, and no collection of your name, email address, or contacts.
  • No advertising, no advertising SDKs, and no "tracking" as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with data brokers or for advertising purposes.
  • No reading of your device's live location.
  • No uploading of photos, videos, photo metadata, or Health data.
  • No use of Photos or Health data to build profiles or contact lists.

Each provider is bound by its own privacy policy and terms to protect that data, and none of them is permitted to use it to advertise to you, to sell it, or to use it for any purpose other than providing the service. Where we share data with a provider, we require the same or equivalent protection for it as this policy describes.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: trips and app data live on your device for as long as you keep them. You can delete individual trips in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device. Neither action touches your photo library or Apple Health.
  • In your iCloud account: synced trip data stays in your iCloud account until you delete it. Deleting a trip in the app deletes its synced copy. Deleting the app does not delete the iCloud copy; to remove it, delete the trips in the app first, or delete Treadline's iCloud data from your device's iCloud settings (see section 6).
  • Diagnostics: telemetry sent to Datadog is retained for a short rolling period (currently no longer than 30 days) and then automatically deleted. Because it carries no name or account, we generally cannot connect stored telemetry to a specific person; if you contact us we will make reasonable efforts to identify and delete data associated with you.
  • Mapping requests: route and place-name requests are processed transiently by the services listed above under their own policies. We keep none of them.
  • Email: correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Your choices

  • Photos: you can deny, limit, or revoke photo library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. The app cannot build trips without it, but that is your call.
  • Apple Health: workout import is optional. You can deny or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Treadline. Already-imported routes remain part of your trips until you delete them.
  • iCloud sync: you can stop Treadline from using iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Saved to iCloud (or "Apps Using iCloud"), and delete Treadline's stored iCloud data from iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Diagnostics: turn them off in the app under Settings → Privacy → Share usage & diagnostics.
  • Deletion requests: for anything we hold ourselves, email feedback@trailsidetable.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise the choices above yourself at any time; for anything else, contact us at the address in section 11. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Because most of your data never reaches us, we may need to explain that we hold nothing that identifies you, or ask for enough information to locate the pseudonymous diagnostics associated with your install.

7.1 European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Processing that takes place entirely on your device, or in your own iCloud account, is under your control and is not something we receive. For the data we do receive, our legal bases are:

  • Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for diagnostics and usage data, which you can withdraw at any time with the in-app switch described above.
  • Performance of the service you requested (Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in operating a working app, for the transient mapping requests that draw your routes and name your stops.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

7.2 California and other US states

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the app. The categories of personal information we collect are identifiers (a random install identifier and IP address), device and usage information, and, for mapping requests, photo-derived geolocation data, each for the purposes described in section 2. Because the app is not a website and does not use tracking cookies, it does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

8. International transfers

We are based in the United States. Diagnostics data is processed by Datadog in the United States. Mapping requests to openrouteservice and the Overpass API are processed in Germany and the European Union. Apple processes iCloud and Maps data under its own policies. Where personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland is transferred to the United States, our providers rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS's built-in app sandboxing and device encryption. Trip data synced to iCloud is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest. All network requests the app makes use encrypted (TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we deliberately minimize what leaves your device so there is very little to protect elsewhere.

10. Children

Treadline is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All photo and Health processing happens on the device regardless of the user's age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app handles, we will update this policy and its effective date, and, for material changes, note it in the app. The current version is always available from the app's Settings screen and at https://johnhann.github.io/treadline-site/privacy.html.
