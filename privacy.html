Treadline Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026 · Trailside Table, LLC

The short version: Treadline turns your photo library into trip movies, and it does that on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded. We don't have user accounts, we don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and we don't track you across apps. The only data that leaves your device is (1) map coordinates used to draw routes and name places, sent without any account or identifier from us, and (2) crash and usage diagnostics that contain no photos, locations, place names, or dates and that we never link to your identity. Those requests do travel to other companies' servers from your device, so those companies see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies.

Who we are

Treadline is made by Trailside Table, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy describes what the Treadline iOS app does with your information. If you have any questions, contact us at feedback@trailsidetable.com.

Your photos stay on your device

Treadline asks for read-only access to your photo library so it can discover trips from your photos' timestamps and location metadata, and play them back as animated map tours. All of this happens on your device:

  • Reading: the app reads photo timestamps, GPS metadata, favorite status, and thumbnails to group photos into trips and stops. It never modifies, adds, or deletes anything in your library — the app is built with a guard that prevents any photo-modification code from shipping.
  • Analysis: the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to pick the best photos for a tour (sharpness, exposure, whether faces are well-lit). This is quality scoring, not facial recognition — it does not identify who is in a photo, and no analysis results leave your device.
  • Storage: your trips (stop locations, place names, dates, photo references, and routes) are stored in a private database on your device.

Your photos, videos, and photo metadata are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The only way imagery leaves the device is when you export a trip movie and choose to share or save it (see "Exported movies" below).

Map coordinates sent to build routes and name places

To draw routes between your stops and give stops readable place names, the app sends coordinates derived from your photos' location metadata to mapping services. The app never reads your device's live location — it has no access to Location Services at all.

  • Apple Maps services (map display, directions, reverse geocoding, place search), covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenRouteService (operated by HeiGIT gGmbH) and the OpenStreetMap Overpass API, used to look up hiking-trail and ferry route geometry for the relevant legs of a trip. See the openrouteservice privacy policy and OSM Foundation privacy policy.

These requests carry coordinates and nothing about you from us: no name, no account, no photo content, and no identifier that links the request to you. We do not store your coordinates on any server of ours. A request still travels from your device to those companies, so they receive your IP address, and what they do with it is governed by their policies linked above rather than by this one — we can describe our own conduct, not theirs.

Diagnostics and usage data

So we can fix crashes and understand which features work, the app sends diagnostic and usage telemetry to Datadog, our monitoring provider. This includes crash reports, performance measurements (for example, how long a route lookup took), which screens are used, and feature-usage events (for example, "a tour was exported").

This telemetry is deliberately scrubbed before it leaves your device. It never includes your photos, photo identifiers, coordinates, place names, trip titles, or trip dates. It is not linked to your identity — there are no accounts, and we never attach a name, email, or other personal identifier. It does include a randomly generated session/device identifier (used only to group events from the same app install), device model and OS version, and the app version, and Datadog receives your IP address with the request. That identifier persists across sessions, so this data is more accurately described as pseudonymous than anonymous: it is not tied to who you are, but events from a single app install can be grouped together. We do not use this data for advertising, do not share it with data brokers, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Exported movies

When you export a trip movie, the video is rendered entirely on your device. It contains your chosen photos and clips, the animated map of your trip, and place-name titles — so treat it like any personal video. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination in the share sheet (Messages, AirDrop, Save to Photos, and so on). We never receive a copy.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, and no collection of your name, email, or contacts.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, and no "tracking" as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with data brokers — ever.
  • No reading of your device's live location.
  • No uploading of photos, videos, or photo metadata.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: trips and app data live only on your device. You can delete individual trips in the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data. This does not touch your photo library.
  • Diagnostics: telemetry sent to Datadog is retained for a limited period for troubleshooting and then automatically deleted. Because it carries no name or account, we generally cannot connect stored telemetry to a specific person; if you contact us we will make reasonable efforts to identify and delete data associated with you.
  • Mapping requests: route and place-name requests are processed transiently by the services listed above under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or revoke photo library access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos (the app can't build trips without it, but that's your call).
  • You can withdraw consent to diagnostics collection or request deletion of your data by contacting us at feedback@trailsidetable.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. Contact us and we will honor applicable requests.

Third parties we work with

We share data only with the service providers named in this policy — Apple Maps services, OpenRouteService (HeiGIT), the OpenStreetMap Overpass API, and Datadog — and only the minimal data described above, solely so they can provide their service to the app. Each is bound by its own privacy policy (linked above) to protect that data; none of them is permitted to use it to advertise to you or to sell it.

Children

Treadline is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All photo processing happens on-device regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app handles — for example, if we add optional iCloud sync for your trips — we will update this policy, adjust the effective date above, and, for material changes, note it in the app. The current version will always be available at https://johnhann.github.io/treadline-site/privacy.html.
